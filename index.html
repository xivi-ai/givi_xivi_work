Problem and insight

Training data is the bottleneck.

The problem

Every non-invasive fetal AI is short of the same data. You cannot collect it without a device, and you cannot validate the device without the data.

The insight

The physics can be computed forward. The data can be generated, and the sensor designed, in simulation on GPUs.

Approach
